About the Project
Smart Marketplace Intelligence Platform
An AI-powered, full-stack operations management system developed by interns at Farmer-to-Market Program to modernize farm monitoring, maintenance, and decision-making.
Vision, Mission & Objectives
🔭
Vision

A Digitally Intelligent Marketplace

To transform Farmer-to-Market Program into a fully data-driven, AI-augmented operation where every decision is backed by real-time intelligence.

🎯
Mission

Empower Operators with AI

Deliver an integrated platform that reduces downtime, prevents failures before they occur, and gives every operator actionable insights at a glance.

📌
Objectives

Measure, Predict, Optimize

Track KPIs in real time, predict marketplace failures using ML, forecast harvest accurately, and provide a unified interface for all marketplace operations.

⚠ Problem Statement

  • Marketplace failures are detected reactively — only after breakdown occurs, causing unplanned downtime.
  • Harvest data is siloed across departments, making holistic analysis difficult for leadership.
  • Maintenance scheduling is largely manual and calendar-based, not condition-based.
  • Order Issue management lacks AI-powered severity classification and trend analysis.
  • No unified digital dashboard for all farm KPIs, quality scores, and logistics consumption.

✅ Proposed Solution

  • ML-powered predictive maintenance using Random Forest & XGBoost on real sensor data.
  • Unified executive dashboard with all KPIs, trends, and AI-generated summaries.
  • Condition-based maintenance planner with work order management and calendar view.
  • AI order issue severity classifier using TF-IDF + Random Forest / DistilBERT.
  • Interactive 3D Digital Twin of the marketplace for visual farm health monitoring.
Key Benefits
⬆

Increased Efficiency

Real-time monitoring and AI insights improve farm availability and throughput by reducing unplanned stoppages.

🔧

Reduced Downtime

Predictive models identify failure risk 7–30 days in advance, enabling proactive maintenance scheduling.

🧠

Better Decision Making

AI Insights Engine combines all model outputs to give leadership a clear, prioritized view of what needs attention.

🛡️

Improved Quality

Quality KPI tracking, order issue trend analysis, and automatic severity classification reduce risk and improve compliance.

💡

Innovation Culture

The Innovation Hub empowers every employee to submit ideas, vote, and contribute to continuous improvement.

🌱

Sustainability Tracking

Sustainability emission dashboard tracks CO₂, water consumption, and logistics intensity to support ESG goals.

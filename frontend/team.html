Problem Statement
🌾

Direct Farmer-to-Market Connect

Problem Statement ID: 26033 · Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices. AgriCentre is a digital marketplace that connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers, with built-in logistics support and AI-driven price intelligence.
